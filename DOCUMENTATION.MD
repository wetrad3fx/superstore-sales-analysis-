Project Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Excel reporting solution designed to analyze sales performance across regions, states, product categories, customer segments, shipping modes, and time periods.

The dashboard converts transactional sales data into key performance indicators (KPIs), PivotTables, charts, and interactive filters, enabling users to quickly identify sales trends and regional/category performance.

The dashboard follows a standard Excel BI approach using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers to provide an interactive reporting experience.

1. Business Objective

The primary objectives of the dashboard are to:

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Compare sales across regions and states.
* Analyze performance by product category and sub-category.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Understand customer segment performance.
* Analyze shipping-mode distribution.
* Provide an interactive tool for management decision-making.



2. Sales by Region

Regional performance shows: West is the highest-performing region, while South generates the lowest sales.


3. Sales by category 

Technology is the largest category by sales, contributing approximately 36.6% of total sales.

4. Sales by State

The dashboard provides state-level sales analysis, allowing users to identify high- and low-performing markets.

California is the leading state in the displayed state-level PivotTable, followed by New York, Texas, Pennsylvania, Washington, and Florida.


5. Customer Segments
Sales are divided into:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

Consumer is the largest segment, followed by Corporate and Home Office.

6. Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers for:

* Region
* Segment
* Category

Users can select one or multiple values to dynamically filter the dashboard.

7. Data Analysis Structure

The underlying workbook uses PivotTables to aggregate transactional sales data into different analytical dimensions, including:

* Region
* State
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Product
* Customer
* Segment
* Ship Mode
* Order Date

8. Key Business Insights

1. West is the strongest region, generating approximately ₦710K in sales.
2. Technology is the leading product category, generating approximately ₦827K.
3. South is the weakest region, with approximately ₦389K in sales.
4. Consumer customers represent the largest customer segment.
5. Monthly sales show stronger performance toward the end of each year.
